Why soil?

The science beneath our feet

Soil is more than dirt. It is a living world of organisms, roots, minerals, water and connections that shape the environments around us. At The Soil Academy, participants look closer, ask questions and discover science through guided, hands-on exploration.

  1. 01

    Observe soil and its living organisms.

  2. 02

    Investigate textures and layers.

  3. 03

    Explore how water moves through soil.

  4. 04

    Discover the connections between soil, food and the environment.

Flagship program

Soil Detectives: The Living World

  • Ages approximately 7–12
  • Hands-on, with microscopes
  • Soil Passport to record discoveries
  • Duration and location confirmed per session

A hands-on soil discovery experience designed for curious children approximately ages 7–12. Participants explore real soil samples, investigate what lives beneath the surface and use tools such as microscopes to look more closely at the world around them. Activities may include guided observation, simple soil investigations and a Soil Passport to record discoveries. Exact activities, duration, capacity and location are confirmed for each session.

Good to know

Questions families and hosts ask

What is The Soil Academy?

A science education and discovery initiative by Noc & Bez Creative Inc. that explores the living world beneath our feet through hands-on experiences.

Who is Soil Detectives designed for?

The flagship experience is designed for approximately ages 7–12. Contact us to discuss suitability for your group.

Can my school or community host a session?

We welcome inquiries from schools, community groups and venues. Sessions depend on fit, logistics and availability.

Where are sessions held?

The Soil Academy is based in Calgary. Locations are confirmed for individual events and partnerships.

How much does a session cost?

Pricing depends on group size, format, materials, location and other requirements. Request a quote.

Can I buy tickets today?

Public ticketing is offered only for confirmed events. You can submit an inquiry to express interest.

Is The Soil Academy a permanent discovery centre?

A dedicated discovery centre is a longer-term aspiration, not a currently operating venue.

How do you handle safety?

Each experience is planned with age-appropriate activities, supervision, venue requirements and safety considerations. Specific arrangements are confirmed with the host.
