The Soil Academy

Explore. Observe. Discover.

The Soil Academy brings soil science to life through hands-on activities, observation and discovery. We invite children, young people and adults to explore soil, investigate its living communities and understand its connections to water, food and the environment.

Hands-on activities

What You Can Explore

  1. 01

    Observe soil and its living organisms.

  2. 02

    Investigate textures and layers.

  3. 03

    Explore how water moves through soil.

  4. 04

    Discover the connections between soil, food and the environment.

Activities vary by session.
