Good to know

Questions, answered

Do I need a fully developed project plan?

No. We can start with an idea, an early-stage initiative or a project already in progress.

Do you work with individuals and organizations?

Yes. Fit depends on project scope, goals, timeline and available capacity.

Can you take over an existing project?

We can assess the current state and propose a clearly defined coordination or recovery scope.

Do you guarantee project success?

No. We provide structured planning, coordination and reporting; results also depend on client decisions, resources and external factors.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on scope, complexity and level of involvement. We provide a proposal before paid work begins.
