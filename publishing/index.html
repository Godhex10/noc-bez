Path A

I Have a Book Idea

For authors with an idea, outline or unfinished draft.

Turn Your Book Idea Into a Plan You Can Follow.

You do not need to have every answer before you begin. We help you clarify your publishing goal, map out the work ahead and create achievable milestones so you can make steady progress on your manuscript.

  • Clarify your intended reader, format, goals and realistic timeline.
  • Create a writing and publishing roadmap with checkpoints.
  • Receive process guidance and scheduled accountability support.
  • Understand when to engage a qualified independent editor.
  • Move into production coordination when the author has a finalized manuscript.

Not included: ghostwriting, manuscript rewriting, developmental editing, copyediting, proofreading or guaranteed publishing outcomes.

Path B

I Have a Manuscript

For authors with a complete draft, edited manuscript or finished files.

Your Manuscript Is Ready for Its Next Chapter.

We help authors organize the production and self-publishing steps, without the confusion of navigating formatting, files and Amazon KDP alone.

  • Identify which publishing inputs and approvals are still outstanding.
  • Prepare a project plan and coordinate with the Noc & Bez formatter/KDP specialist as scoped.
  • Explain independent editing, cover design and illustration options when required.
  • Guide metadata, book description, categories, pricing and KDP account decisions.
  • Assist with file upload, preview checks and author-approved submission.
  • Provide a final publishing handover checklist.

Not included unless separately quoted: professional editing, proofreading, illustration, cover design, print costs, advertising, guaranteed approval or book sales.

Good to know

Questions authors ask

Do I need a finished manuscript?

No. We offer planning and accountability for idea-stage authors and publishing support for authors with completed manuscripts.

Do you edit manuscripts?

Not in-house. We can explain the types of editing and help you identify independent professionals; editing is a separate service and cost.

Can you publish my book on Amazon?

We provide Amazon KDP guidance and upload assistance for eligible, publication-ready books. Amazon determines acceptance and availability.

Who owns my book and royalties?

For guided self-publishing services, authors retain their rights and control their own KDP accounts and royalties, subject to their signed agreement.

Do you guarantee sales or bestseller status?

No. Book sales depend on many factors. We help with a clear publishing process, not guaranteed commercial outcomes.

What about illustrations and cover design?

These can be supplied by the author or separately arranged with qualified professionals, depending on project scope.

How long does publishing take?

It varies by manuscript readiness, specialist availability, revisions and Amazon review. We provide a realistic timeline after intake.

Can I work with you if I am outside Calgary?

Remote guidance and KDP assistance may be available; confirm location and service eligibility during inquiry.
