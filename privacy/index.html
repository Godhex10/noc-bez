Privacy

How we use the information you send us through this website.

What we collect

When you send an inquiry through one of our forms (Project & Business Solutions, Publishing, The Soil Academy or Contact), we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number (if you choose to give it), your organization, and your answers about your project, book or event.

The Soil Academy form asks only for general age ranges. We do not ask for children’s names or dates of birth.

We do not ask for payment details, passwords or files, and you do not need an account to use this website.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to your inquiry and to talk with you about the service you asked about.

We only send you news and updates if you tick the separate box asking for them. We do not sell, rent or share your details with anyone for marketing.

Where it goes

Your inquiry is sent by email to our business inbox, and a copy is stored securely on our website so we can follow up. Only Noc & Bez can see it, using a password-protected editor.

We also send you one email confirming that we received your inquiry.

We keep inquiries only for as long as we need them to respond and follow up with you, and then delete them.

Booking an appointment

The Make an Appointment buttons open our booking page on Calendly, a separate service. Details you enter there are handled under Calendly’s own privacy policy.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies for visitors. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and this website does not use analytics.

If you arrive from one of our advertisements, we note which advertisement it was with your inquiry, so we know how you found us.

Other websites

Our website links to other websites, such as our social media pages, our booking page and the stores where our books are sold. Those websites have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Your choices

You can ask us what details we hold about you, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them. Just email us at the address below.

Contact us

If you have any questions about your information, email us at brichard@nocnbez.com or use our Contact page.
