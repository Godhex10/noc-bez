Founder

Bernice Richard

Project Management, Publishing & Science Education

Bernice Richard is an entrepreneur, author and project management professional with a background in biochemistry. She founded Noc & Bez Creative to help people develop ideas, share their stories and discover new possibilities through project management, publishing and education.

As a self-published author, Bernice understands the decisions involved in bringing a book to readers. She supports authors through that process while also leading The Soil Academy, a program that makes soil science accessible through hands-on discovery.

Her work brings together business leadership, creative expression and a commitment to learning.

Amazon Seller Central & KDP Expert

Sami Hafeez

Sami Hafeez is an Amazon Seller Central and Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) expert with over eight years of experience in e-commerce and Amazon publishing. He holds a master's degree in Engineering Management.

Having worked with over 1,100 clients worldwide, Sami has experience across a diverse range of products and book genres. He helps authors and sellers navigate the publishing and selling process, from product research and listing optimisation to publishing and marketing on Amazon.

At Noc & Bez Creative, Sami supports book formatting, Amazon keyword research and publishing preparation.

Website Design & Development

Prince Ebere

Prince Ebere creates websites that help authors and businesses present their work online.

For businesses, he builds websites that showcase their services and work, with tools to update content and receive enquiries. For authors, his work brings together biographies, book showcases, purchase links and contact information in one accessible place. He focuses on clear navigation, readable layouts and designs that work across phones, tablets and computers.

Prince helps turn each client's ideas into a website they can use to introduce their work and connect with their audience.

Design Engineer & Product Designer

Nathan Onimiya

Nathan Onimiya is a design engineer and product designer with over a decade in product design. He turns complex workflows into clear interfaces, reusable systems and working code.

His background is in graphic and product design, and he now works across the interface and the code behind it. He has worked with organisations including McKinsey & Company, MTN, Nedbank and United Arab Bank.

Noc & Bez Creative brings together project management support, publishing services and hands-on science education.
